Captions

We will approach this problem from first principles.

Gestures

Tap a word to pause and look it up. Tap the same word again to close and resume. A different word stays paused and looks up the new one.

AI fallback

Up to three OpenAI-compatible providers, five models each. Word tap pauses and tries Yomitan. The small AI button on the caption bar asks the model. Empty slots are skipped.

Backup

Clear caption cache reloads tracks for the open YouTube tab (fixes a video stuck on the wrong language). Export is plain JSON: fonts, colors, API keys, prompts, toggles.
