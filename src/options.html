v1.1.0 — YouTube captions for touch immersion. No translation.

We will approach this problem from first principles.

Gestures

Tap a word to pause and look it up. Tap the same word again to close and resume. A different word stays paused and looks up the new one.

AI fallback

OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions. Grok / xAI uses the same shape: base https://api.x.ai/v1. The switch unlocks only after a successful test.
